Training Services

Finding the most suitable candidate can be time consuming and challenging. Choosing the wrong candidate can be all too easy but extremely disruptive and costly in the long run. Using CYMS’s proven candidate-rating system helps to quantify the strengths and weaknesses of each potential new crew member. The right crew member can only be found if he or she is interviewed by the right panel of interviewers. Our team are all highly experienced ex-senior crew members so fully understand the requirements of the role. This allows us to build a focused picture of not only how each candidate would perform, but also how they will integrate into such a dynamic working environment.
