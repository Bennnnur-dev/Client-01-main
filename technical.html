Technical Management

Understanding the needs of complex technical systems ranging from the most advanced engine room automation systems to high end VSAT communication arrays provides reassurance to owners that their assets are being properly and efficiently maintained. Ensuring compliance with Class and Flag rules and providing detailed forecasting on projected survey costs, CYMS can help demystify the long-term costs associated with keeping a fleet in operation.
